Two things sit at the centre of my work: measuring how cities heat, flood and grow, and turning that measurement into plans a government can build.

I hold a Bachelor of Urban and Regional Planning from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology, where I finished 10th in my cohort. Since then I have split my time between peer-reviewed research on urban heat islands and full-scale consultancy for Bangladeshi government agencies.

At Sheltech Consultants I run the GIS and remote sensing side of large government master plans — an 8,080-acre World Bank-backed coastal aquaculture estate in Cox's Bazar, and long-horizon town plans for three upazilas under LGED.

What I work on

  • Urban heat island dynamics, surface and atmospheric
  • Landsat 8, MODIS and Sentinel-1 SAR analysis
  • Machine learning for spatial prediction and air quality
  • Urban form metrics and land cover change
  • Flood inundation mapping and coastal resilience
  • Drought, landslide and disaster risk assessment
  • Evidence-based master planning for South Asian cities
