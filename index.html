## 📚 Publications & Academic Research My research centers on applying geospatial machine learning, remote sensing datasets, and empirical modeling to urban climatology, air quality, and vulnerable coastal socio-ecological systems. ### 📄 Peer-Reviewed Manuscripts & Working Papers 1. **Advanced Environmental Diagnostics & Predictive Modeling in Coastal Aquaculture** - **Focus:** Multi-parametric assessment of soil biogeochemistry, estuarine water quality indices, and small-data machine learning forecasting for sustainable aquaculture ecosystems. - **Study Area:** Chakaria Coastal Belt, Cox’s Bazar. - **Methodology:** Multivariate statistics, spatial interpolation, predictive ML modeling. 2. **Urban Heat Island (UHI) Dynamics & Land Surface Temperature (LST) Variations** - **Focus:** Spatio-temporal evaluation of surface temperature trends in relation to urban form and normalized difference vegetation/built-up indices (NDVI/NDBI). - **Data & Tools:** Multi-temporal Landsat 8/9 TIRS & OLI imagery, GEE, statistical spatial regression. - **Scope:** Major metropolitan regions (Dhaka & Chittagong). 3. **High-Resolution Air Quality Vulnerability Mapping via Machine Learning** - **Focus:** Modeling tropospheric pollutant concentrations (NO₂, PM2.5, aerosols) against demographic exposure and built-environment density. - **Data & Tools:** Sentinel-5P TROPOMI, MODIS aerosol optical depth, Random Forest (RF) regression in Python/R. 4. **Multi-Modal Transit Integration & Walkability Assessment in Secondary Towns** - **Focus:** Micro-level pedestrian accessibility modeling and connectivity optimization in emerging municipal centers under national infrastructure investment programs.
